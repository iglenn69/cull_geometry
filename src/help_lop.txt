= Cull Geometry =

#type: node
#context: lop
#internal: cull_geometry_lop
#icon: SOP/blast

"""Culls USD geometry outside a camera's view frustum."""

Wraps a SOP Modify LOP that runs the Cull Geometry SOP on the selected
primitives, using a camera from the input stage.

With __Input Packed Geometry__ on, each matched USD primitive is culled as a
whole using its bounds. With it off, the primitives are unpacked to polygons
and individual points or faces are culled.

@parameters

Primitives:
    #id: primpattern
    USD primitives to cull.

Camera:
    #id: camera
    USD camera prim on the input stage. Empty uses the first camera found.

Cull Occluded:
    #id: occlusion
    Also cull primitives hidden behind other matched primitives. Only the
    primitives matched by __Primitives__ act as occluders.

Other parameters:
    See the Cull Geometry SOP.
